Our History



History Of The Website



When creating a website we decided to look at features and the target audience that would be engaged with our website. From this we created a website that had a focus on users that can log into a system for the two primary features of our website, which is based around e-commerce, where users can browse different caravans and learn of their history or brand and for others, they can use this website as a way to setup communication with sellers who have put up their caravans on the site for sale. When designing this website we wanted to allow the sit to also be accessible for multiple users, which we reflect in our design, by using this easy to read font and highly contrasting colours, with specific words or functions sticking out more so we can help users who may suffer from colour blindness or may be hard of sight, or may have learning difficulties navigate our website and enjoy just as much as any other user! This leads us to today, where we are still developing and looking for improvements in our website to help create a more diverse and unique experience for all caravan fans



History Of Us (The Developers)

The background of us is that we are a group of uni students that banded together to create this website together, for some of us it was a new experience jumping into coding using CSS, HTML and PHP, however due to our experience in coding languages such as python and java, we were able to successfully work on this project together and individually create different functions and pages to package together into one website, for example Fred has been the creator of the account page and how you manage the caravans, by adding editing and deleting them. Dan has ben the creator of the central home page and is responsible for the daily updates that users receive when logging in, Ioan has been the front for the store, showing listings and details of caravans that can be put up for sale and establishing a base of contacts between users, and finally George who has been programming the register, login and the about us pages for information and security in the site, while undertaking this project however we all developed skills to help improve the design and the intuitiveness of the website, and also improved our communication skills to help better give feedback to each other and make suggestions for improvements
