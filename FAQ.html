Frequently Asked Questions

Here is where we contain some of our frequently asked questions, if you have any questions that are not present here feel free to email us at MyCaravan@Gmail.com!



Questions About The Website



Question: Where can I log out?

Answer: You can log out on the Account section of the website, we originally had it so that users could log out from the nav bar, however we found that in testing clicking the logout function was a common accident and caused mistakes



Question: Is there any filters in place for the store page so i can look for a certain brand/make of caravan?

Answer: While we do not have any in place right now we could add such features for filters as well as a search bar in the future!



Question: Is there anyway I can contact the buyers through this website?

Answer: Due to us being relatively small scale, we only put a contact of email or phone number for potential buyers to contact the owner, however if we grow in scale we may provide a platform for this.



Questions About Your Data



Question: Is my data secure on this website?

Answer: Yes! We have databases that are secured by hashing for certain private information such as passwords



Question: What is this data used for?

We only use data that is relevant to our website, and use it for communication purposes and for creating profiles on our website



Question: Are you going to sell our data?

Answer: No we aren't the data we have is for us and only us, if you delete your account we will delete all remaining data manually from the system. You can see what data we have on you on the Account Page
